Luma Living Residence

PROJECT OVERVIEW
Location Bengaluru, India
Project Type Adaptive Reuse & Modernization
Scope Structural Retrofit & Environmental Optimization
Status Completed

This project integrates open collaboration zones with quieter focus areas. Clean lines, modular furniture, and integrated lighting create a professional environment that encourages productivity while remaining adaptable to different team sizes and working styles.

THE APPROACH

Acoustics, Privacy & Spatial Order

The key challenge was creating flexibility without compromising acoustic comfort or visual order. The layout needed to support collaboration while ensuring privacy, requiring careful zoning, material selection, and lighting strategies to manage noise and movement.

THE OUTCOME

Flexible Framework & Performance

The finished space supports a range of work modes within a cohesive design framework. Clear zoning and adaptable elements allow the space to evolve, delivering an environment that feels efficient, balanced, and comfortable for daily use.
